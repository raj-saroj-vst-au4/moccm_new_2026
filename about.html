About the Centre

Industry-defined. Academia-led. Action-focused.

Vision

India's premier innovation engine for Capital Markets: convening high-impact collaboration, delivering actionable research, and supplying future-ready talent to the global financial services industry.

Innovation Engine

Four stages that feed one another. What the Centre returns in the last stage fuels the next round of innovation calls.

  1. 1

    Sourcing: Innovation Calls

    “We don't guess problems. We convene them.”

    Research agendas defined by real-world friction points and commercial opportunities.

  2. 2

    Building: Applied Research

    “From working papers to working prototypes.”

    Develop deployable prototypes, intellectual property (IP), and policy frameworks.

  3. 3

    Empowering: Future-Ready Talent

    “Curriculum built on live challenges.”

    Academic programs integrated with live research projects.

  4. 4

    Scaling: Returning Value

    “Deliver trained professionals, spin-off startups, and validated technology.”

    Self-sustaining model that fuels the next cycle of innovation.

The Centre

A shared resource base

The Centre will house all the required resources - expertise, data, analytical tools, software, use cases, simulations, etc. It will be the go-to place for any support in research, training, and policy formulation in the country.

International collaboration

The Centre will enter into collaborative agreements with similar entities in other geographies for mutual benefit - faculty and student exchange, joint conferences/workshops, joint research projects, joint academic programmes, etc.

Support for startups

The Centre will also encourage and promote startups in the emerging areas such as Fintech, AI/ML in Banking and Finance, Insuretech, Wealth Management, Regtech, Finance Apps, Financial Analytics, Blockchain, Digital Currency, etc.

Faculty Positions

Educational Qualifications All grades

Ph.D. with a first class or equivalent (at least 60% marks or 6.5 CPI on a scale of 10) at the preceding degree level in Financial Markets, Financial Economics, Finance, Banking, Insurance, Financial Analytics, Fintech, Application of AI/ML in Finance, etc.

Assistant Professor Grade I & II

Experience

Grade I: Minimum of three years post-Ph.D. teaching/research/professional experience of which at least one year should be post-doctoral research and/or teaching experience in a University/College/an Institute/industry.

Grade II: Candidates with less than three years post-doctoral experience.

Publications
Minimum of 3 papers in reputed journals, with at least 1 in B or higher-level journals as per ABDC ranking list or ABS level 2+ or equivalent.

Associate Professor

Experience
Minimum of 6 years post-Ph.D. teaching/research/professional experience, with at least 3 years at Assistant Professor Grade I level or equivalent.
Publications
Minimum of 8 papers in reputed journals, with at least 4 in B or higher-level journals as per ABDC ranking list or ABS level 2+ or equivalent.
Teaching & Ph.D. Guidance
Should have taught MBA/Masters level courses and supervised at least one Ph.D. student.

Professor

Experience
Minimum of 10 years post-Ph.D. teaching/research/professional experience, with at least 4 years at Associate Professor level or equivalent.
Publications
Minimum of 12 papers in reputed journals, with at least 6 in B or higher-level journals as per ABDC ranking list or ABS level 2+ or equivalent.
Ph.D. Guidance
Should have supervised at least two Ph.D. students who have graduated.

Desirable Criteria for Associate Professor and Professor

  • Experience in a business school or reputed university
  • Publications in top-tier journals (A* or A level journals)
  • Evidence of securing research grants
  • International collaborations and visiting positions
  • Industry consulting experience
  • Awards and recognitions in the field
